Build the morning heads-up for a field tech. This run is 11:30 UTC, which is 6:30am America/New_York in standard time and 7:30am in daylight time. Monday through Saturday.

Search work email for today's jobs and tomorrow morning's jobs. Look for service calls, dispatch, estimates, and addresses. Skip newsletters, receipts, and mail that is not a job.

Write a brief the tech can read in the truck. For each job give the time, customer, address, the complaint, equipment if the email names it, access notes, and parts or filters to grab. Then list conflicts, missing addresses, and anything that needs a callback before arrival.

Do not send, delete, forward, or relabel any email. If Gmail tools are not available, say the mail connection is missing and stop. Do not invent jobs.
