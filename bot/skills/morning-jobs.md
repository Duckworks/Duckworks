# Morning jobs

1. Use the Gmail search and read tools. Search today and tomorrow morning for service calls, dispatch, estimates, and addresses.
2. Skip newsletters, receipts, and anything that is not a job.
3. Write a truck brief. For each job: time, customer, address, complaint, equipment named in the mail, access notes, and parts or filters to grab.
4. End with conflicts, missing addresses, and callbacks to make before arrival.
5. Do not send, delete, forward, or relabel mail.
6. If the Gmail tools are missing, say the mail connection is missing and stop. Do not invent jobs.
